WorldArts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

WorldArts is a creative platform designed to showcase and support artists and their works. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use WorldArts.

1. Information We May Collect

WorldArts may receive information needed to authenticate users, operate the application, display artwork, process transactions, and maintain application security. This may include information provided through Pi Network authentication and information associated with artwork or artist profiles.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve WorldArts, authenticate users, process and verify payments, display content, prevent abuse, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. Payments

WorldArts is designed to support payments in Pi Network and WART. Payment-related information is used only as necessary to initiate, verify, complete, and record transactions.

4. Third-Party Services

WorldArts may use third-party services such as Pi Network and hosting or infrastructure providers. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply to information handled by those services.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate WorldArts, comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, after which it may be removed or anonymized.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the information involved and applicable law, users may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information associated with their account or profile. Requests can be made through the contact information provided below.

7. Children's Privacy

WorldArts is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to use of such services, and WorldArts does not knowingly collect information from such individuals.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning WorldArts, please contact the WorldArts administrator through the contact information provided by the application.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WorldArts changes or adds features. The updated version will be published on this page.
